# cob-studio

## What is this app?

<!-- Describe the app in a sentence or two. What does it do? Who is it for? -->
This is a e-commerce app for an artist named John Orth. The company is called Congress of Beauty and is COB for short. The url will be cob-studio.com.

It will sell original artwork, which includes framed painting and drawings. It will also include cast series like those of mushrooms which are tabletop sculpture (around 8" wide and 4" tall).

The basis of the app will be this: https://www.whatsupton.com/. For now, just include a gradient filled box for each of the image spaces. Those will be included later. 

## Goals

<!-- What are you trying to accomplish? What does success look like? -->

I want the app to allow users to view the work, find out about the studio and then make purchases. I'm assuming Stripe would be the easiest backend for that, but I'm open.
